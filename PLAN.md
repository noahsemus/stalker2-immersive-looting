# ImmersiveLooting — plan

When Skif picks something up, his left arm reaches out, the item travels into his left hand, and he pulls it in,
instead of the item vanishing. It works with a weapon in hand: the left hand leaves the grip, grabs, and returns.

Research and citations: [zonekit/research/2026-10-09-pickup-and-left-hand-research.md](zonekit/research/2026-10-09-pickup-and-left-hand-research.md).

## How the game does it today
- Loose items (F on an item lying in the world) are picked up instantly with no animation. Every loose item is one
  of two generic actors (`BP_StaticItemContainer` / `BP_SkeletalItemContainer`) holding a `MeshComponent`.
- Artifacts (two-handed artifact pickup) and mutant parts (knife looting) are already animated in vanilla. NPC
  corpses and stashes are looted in the loot window; nothing moves in the world.
- The game ships an unused **left-hand grab while holding a rifle**: `MG_fp_ak74_weapon_grab` on
  `fp_ar_weapon_grab` (1.67 s, `LeftHand` slot, only the left arm moves, camera still, hand IK to the foregrip
  switched off and back on by notifies, grab at ~0.57 s, hand to the chest at ~0.92 s). The firearm anim
  collections even have a `PickUpItem` field for it, filled only for the grenade-launcher AK / gp37 variants.
- Every weapon layer (rifles, pistols, shotguns, bare hands, knife, bolt, grenades, detector) has the same
  `LeftHand` slot with a left-arm mask, so one montage played in that slot animates the left arm over any stance.

## Approach (no overrides; everything is NewContent)
A runtime host as in our other mods (`harness/docs/runtime-host.md`): `BP_ImmLootSubsystem` (ModWorldSubsystem) →
`BP_ImmLootActor`, plus our own montage and a small "fake item" actor.

1. **See the pickup coming.** Each tick: `PC.GetInteractionTarget` → owner. If it is a loose item
   (`InteractableItemContainer`), remember the actor, its mesh asset and world transform. When that actor vanishes
   right after being the target (destroyed / invalid / hidden within a few frames), it was picked up.
2. **Play the reach.** `Montage_Play` on the player mesh's anim instance with `AM_ImmLoot_Grab`: a copy of
   `MG_fp_ak74_weapon_grab` in our plugin (new asset, references the vanilla sequence) with the two hand-IK
   notifies kept and the two native `AttachItem` notifies removed. Play rate adjustable (target well under 1 s
   total; the motion is 1.67 s at 1.0).
3. **Fly the item into the hand.** Spawn `BP_ImmLootItem` (a mesh actor with the remembered mesh) at the item's
   spot, move it to the left hand bone `jnt_l_weapon` so it arrives at the grab frame, attach it there, and hide it
   as the hand comes back to the chest (the "pocketing" frame, ~0.8-0.9 s).
4. **Stay out of the way.** Skip (item just vanishes, vanilla) while sprinting, aiming down sights, reloading or any
   `MainActionSlot` action, another left-hand action (flashlight, NVG, injector, bolt throw, detector equip), in
   dialogue / trade / cutscenes. A new pickup during a reach restarts it; never queue. Never triggered by inventory
   changes (auto-loot mods, Take All).
5. **Recover.** If our montage is cut before its "hand back on the grip" notify, play
   `MG_fp_return_left_hand_on_item` so the hand returns to the weapon.

| Layer | Change | Ships as | Conflicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subsystem + actor + montage copy + fake-item actor | NewContent | None (nobody installed touches these; the vanilla grab sequence is only referenced) |
| 2 | Subsystem data asset (`Autogenerated_*_WorldSubsystemData`) | override pak (generated, unique) | None |
| - | Not planned: filling `PickUpItem` in weapon anim collections, giving the item container BP an anim collection | override | OXA overrides 38 collections; any looting mod touching the container BP |

## Builds
The tester's call (2026-10-09): **build 1 is the whole feature** (detection, reach, flying copy, skips, recovery),
not staged parts. A dev probe (`zonekit/tools/probe/ImmLootProbe`, UE4SS Lua) rides along and logs every pickup,
reach, skip reason and copy, so a failure can be split without extra runs. After build 1, fixes go one per cook.
1. Everything in "Approach" above. Defaults until the tester decides: loose items only, guns and big items get
   the same grab, PlayRate 1.4 (grab ~0.4 s, item pocketed ~0.57 s, hand back on the grip ~0.85 s).
2. Fixes from build 1 (likely: FOV mismatch of the flying copy vs the first-person hand, big items, timing).
3. Compatibility pass: Player Gestures (lowered stance, sit anywhere), ImmersiveCampfires, ImmersiveDialogue.
4. Optional: MCM add-on (on/off, speed) as a separate plugin, only if the tester wants settings.

## Corpses and stashes (tester, 2026-10-09)
After the player closes a corpse's or a stash's loot window, if they took at least one item from it, the same
left-hand grab plays once (no flying copy: there is no item in the world). The game has no BP-visible
"inventory changed" or "loot window" event, so (research: `zonekit/research/2026-10-09-loot-window-research.md`):
- **Open / closed**: the input context `IMC_Inventory` is applied (backpack, corpse and stash share the
  `W_Inventory` view; traders use `W_Trade` / `IMC_Trade`). Closed = context gone for 0.25 s and the view hidden or
  out of the viewport (a split-stack popup keeps the view shown), 3 s fail-safe.
- **Which container**: the interaction target from the tick before opening: `DeadBody` = corpse, owner an
  `InteractableItemContainer` = stash (world stashes are subclasses of the item-container actors); the player's own
  stash (`PlayerStash`), mutants and the I-key backpack (`IsUsingBackpack`) are ignored.
- **Took something**: the window's two `CurrentWeightText` widgets (player, loot), found once per window by path
  among all `W_Text` widgets, read as numbers: player heavier or loot lighter by more than 0.04 kg, or more money,
  against a baseline taken in the first 0.3 s. Blind spots: weightless items (notes, flash drives) unless money
  changes; mods that rebuild the inventory layout (then no loot reach, nothing else breaks).
