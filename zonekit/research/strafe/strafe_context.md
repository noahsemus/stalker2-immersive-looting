## The bug (tester, in game)
Empty hands, Skif picks up a loose item: our grab animation (left hand reaches to the item) plays. If the player
strafes left/right (A/D) during the pickup, the reaching hand swings wildly across the screen. Standing still it
looks right. The tester: "the camera doesn't tilt, the player controls the camera. It's the body that rotates and the
arm rotates with the body." The camera/view does NOT turn while strafing.

## How the pickup plays now
- `AS_ImmLoot_GrabBHAdd` (standing) / `AS_ImmLoot_GrabBHAddCrouch` (crouched): LOCAL-SPACE ADDITIVE tables
  (AAT_LocalSpaceBase, ref = their own frame 0 = `fp_bh_idle_stand` frame 0 / crouch idle frame 0), keyed on
  jnt_l_arm / jnt_l_fore_arm / jnt_l_hand + left fingers; segments per item direction. Played as a dynamic montage in
  slot `FullBody` (AnimBP_Player, evaluated after the whole linked bh layer and IK).
- The kit is at `G:\Epic Games\STALKER2ZoneKit`; game animations under `/Game/_STALKER2/Animations/Player/...`
  (bh = bare hands; e.g. `/Game/_STALKER2/Animations/Player/AnimSequences/bh/stand/fp_bh_idle_stand`).

## Measured facts
- bh strafe/run additives (`AS_fp_bh_walk_left_add`, `_right_add`, `fp_bh_run_left_add`, ...) are
  AAT_RotationOffsetMeshSpace, ref = `fp_bh_idle_stand` frame 0. Component-space yaw of bones' rest-forward axis:
  walk_left: hips -90, spine_03 -93, neck/head -93, L shoulder -93; walk_right: hips +90, spine_03 +87, L shoulder +77;
  run_left: hips -95, spine_03 -41..-77, neck/head ~-54, L shoulder -40..-78. Inside walk_left the left hand also moves
  20->40 cm relative to jnt_camera between frames (arm swing in the walk cycle).
- In game while strafing: camera and mesh component do not turn (probe: 0 deg). Left shoulder yaw (component space,
  X axis) 80-83 deg at rest, swings up to +-75 deg while strafing during our pickup.
- The camera hangs off `jnt_camera` (child of jnt_root). Arm bones do not follow view pitch; only jnt_camera pitches
  (look-vertical additive `ApplyMeshSpaceAdditive_20` near the bh layer output).

## What was tried (all failed or partial)
- Builds 49-53: measuring body/hips/upper-back turn (all read 0 because of a gating bug or wrong bone).
- Build 55 (written, never tested): `AS_ImmLoot_Twist` (local additive on jnt_spine_01 only, frame 90+phi = turn phi
  about vertical, 181 frames, ref frame 90) in DefaultSlot, per tick phi from the L shoulder yaw (feedback loop).
  Reviewer: left strafes push the montage to its end within 2 frames (blend-in overshoot) -> it auto-ends; walk-cycle
  arm swing remains (~10 cm); crouch axis wrong; cuts the game's LeftHand montages (same group).
- Earlier (builds ~30): `AS_ImmLoot_PickPose` in MainActionSlot (a held "item in hand" pose): tester said it hijacked
  the camera ("camera moves up slightly... tippy toes") and/or no animation played. A full, non-additive pose in a slot
  also overrides jnt_camera unless masked.
- Reviewer's suggested alternative: hold `fp_bh_idle_stand` frame 0 (crouch: crouch idle frame 0) as a non-additive
  constant sequence in MainActionSlot during the pickup (legs keep walking via legs_blend_mask). Unverified: whether
  legs_blend_mask gives hips/spine weight 0, what it does to jnt_camera / jnt_root, mesh-space vs local blend.
